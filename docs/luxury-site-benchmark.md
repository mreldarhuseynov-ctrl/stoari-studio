# STOARI — comparison with Marbella luxury property sites

Reviewed 5 October 2026 for the existing STOARI landing page. The brief is to
sell media services clearly while preserving the approved identity, films,
particle wordmark and white palette. The owner prefers square containers and
compact, illustrated service rows.

## Reference observations

- [Homerun Brokers](https://www.homerunmarbella.com/) places property imagery,
  named listings, area information and its team alongside its marketing process.
  Contact options are direct: phone and email. Its customer evidence concerns
  actual listings and service experience.
- [Drumelia](https://www.drumelia.com/) gives property imagery and named team
  members prominent roles, explains its departments and connects selling with
  its marketing expertise. Property and contact actions are explicit.
- [Pure Living Properties](https://www.purelivingproperties.com/) positions its
  offer around Marbella’s Golden Mile and property discovery.

This is a qualitative comparison of published page structure and content,
not conversion-rate data or proof that a particular corner radius sells better.

## Application to STOARI

Use STOARI’s real films and selected projects as the evidence. Keep services
and inclusions readable next to prices and direct enquiry options. Preserve the
original serif headings, brand animation and ivory/ink palette. Avoid adding
unsupported awards, sales promises or fabricated testimonials.

Use square corners consistently on media, pricing, service containers and
action buttons, following the owner’s preference. Compact service rows replace
132px minimum heights and separate rounded boxes: shared thin separators, a
visible image background, title and description. Desktop descriptions sit
beside titles; mobile descriptions stack with a small gap. Reduce the gap
before the visualisation process, while keeping readable line-height.

The hero film and brand animation need to coexist without playback stutter.
Performance and source-video checks are separate from visual taste: keep the
animation’s recognizable shapes and verify playback before altering footage.
