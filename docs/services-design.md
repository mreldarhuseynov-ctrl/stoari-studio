# STOARI — service section refinement

The section should let an estate agent understand the five services immediately,
with enough photographic detail to judge the studio's work. The owner requested
a quieter composition without faded image backgrounds, decorative lines or an
AI-template appearance.

UI UX Pro Max informed the responsive grid, image space reservation, 16 px body
copy and heading hierarchy. Impeccable's distill and quieter references informed
the removal of cosmetic borders, repeated controls and unnecessary copy. The
existing ivory, ink, self-hosted fonts and square corners remain the authority;
the skill's generic colour/font suggestions were not substituted for the brand.

Pinterest references inspected visually:

- [Soulful Designs Studio — luxury brand services](https://ca.pinterest.com/pin/elegant-modern-luxury-website-brand-design-for-photographers-luxury-brands-interior-designers--952300283709479968/)
- [Editorial fashion website composition](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/26951297765822187/)

These informed the use of substantial photography and simple captions. Their
overlapping type and decorative collage treatments were not copied, since they
would recreate the owner's readability problem. Reference images are not used
in the site: all five photographs come from the existing STOARI portfolio.

The final section shows five portrait images with headings and short descriptions
on desktop, three columns on smaller desktop/tablet widths, and two columns on
phones. The last mobile service spans the row with image and copy side by side.
Below 380 px, all entries become image/text pairs so long words remain readable
without arbitrary word breaks or horizontal overflow.
There are no decorative borders, card backgrounds, accordion controls or hidden
descriptions. The non-hero particle drawing is hidden; the existing animated
STOARI identity remains visible over the hero. Service copy is shortened in EN,
ES and RU without adding offers or promises. AI calls remain identified as a
real CRM feature; this refinement changes presentation rather than disguising
the nature of an offered service.
